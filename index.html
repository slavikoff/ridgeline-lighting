Why us

The parts that matter when someone is on a ladder above your house

Questions

The ones people actually ask

Who owns the lights?

You do. The hardware is yours after the first season, which is why year two costs 30% less — you're paying for labour, not product. We store it for you at no charge between seasons.

When should I book?

September and October. Installation weeks fill in the order deposits arrive, and the early rate closes October 15. By late November we're generally booked and quoting for the following year.

What if a section goes dark in December?

Call us and we come out. No charge, for the whole season, however many times it happens. It's included in every package rather than sold as a maintenance add-on.

Will this damage my roof or gutters?

No. We use non-invasive ridge and gutter clips exclusively — no staples, nails or adhesive — and our crews work from ladders rather than walking on your roof.

Can you work on a steep or three-storey roof?

Often, yes, and we'll tell you honestly on the design visit if we can't. Some pitches aren't safely reachable from a ladder, and we'd rather decline the job than put someone on a 12/12 in December.

Do I have to be home for the install?

No. Once the design is approved we only need access to an exterior outlet. Most customers come home to a finished house.

Is permanent lighting worth it over seasonal?

If you plan to light your house for more than three or four seasons, the maths usually favours permanent — you stop paying labour every year and it works for every holiday, not just December. We'll run both numbers for you side by side.

Do you do commercial work?

Yes, including certificates of insurance, scheduled install windows and multi-site contracts. Commercial quoting starts in August, so reach out early.
