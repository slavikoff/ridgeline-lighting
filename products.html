Choosing a bulb

Actual sizes, side by side

Drawn to true relative scale. From the street, bulb size matters more than colour — a C9 reads as a decorated house from three doors down, while 5mm reads as a clean outline from the kerb.

C9 on rooflines, C7 on smaller homes and detail runs, globes on patios and pergolas, C6 and G12 on trees and shrubs, M5 and 5mm for wraps, nets and fine work.

Bulb spacing

The choice that changes the look most, and the one nobody explains

Same bulb, same colour, three different houses. Tighter spacing means more bulbs per foot, which means more product cost — this is where a cheap quote and an expensive one usually differ, not in the lights themselves.

8-inch spacing

Dense, continuous and premium. Best on short rooflines and detail work where the run is visible up close. Roughly 1.5 bulbs per foot.

12-inch spacing

Our default. Reads as a clean, even line from the street on a standard two-storey and balances cost against coverage. One bulb per foot.

15-inch spacing

Economical and better suited to long commercial runs, tall peaks and distant viewing. Fewer bulbs, wider gaps, softer effect up close.

Warm white and cool white are not interchangeable

Colour-specified LEDs held to a fixed Kelvin range, so every strand on your house matches — and still matches the strand we bring back next year.
